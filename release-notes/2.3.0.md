- Subscriptions and the Business Plan now use the public subscription service (`subscription.bitcoin-safe.org`). Plans, status, management links and the store are read from the service.
- Plugins declare the subscription product they sell in their manifest (`btcpay.product_id`, e.g. `schedule`). Plugins built for the previous subscription format need an update.
- Subscriptions follow the subscription product, not the plan. When the service moves a product to a new plan, existing subscriptions keep their access and management link.
- Donation invoices take their store from the subscription service.
- Removed the unused BTCPay pay-button address fetcher.
