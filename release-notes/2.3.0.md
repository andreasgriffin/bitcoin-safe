### 📅 [Schedule plugin](https://bitcoin-safe.org/en/plugins/schedule/)
**For businesses and organizations:** pay employees or manage any regular or future obligation with a fixed amount, in any currency.

- 📊 Keep track of future payouts
- ⏰ Get notified when a payment is overdue
- ⚡ Batch payouts with a single click
- 💾 Save and manage recipient addresses (with optional PGP-signed messages)

### 🔔 [Notifications plugin](https://bitcoin-safe.org/en/plugins/notifications/)
Stay in the loop on wallet activity through the channels that work for you. Get alerts about wallet transactions, scheduled payments, and more via:

- 🔗 Webhook
- 🟣 Nostr
- 💬 Telegram
- 📆 Calendars (CalDAV)
- ✉️ Email

### 🛡️ More Robust Transaction Broadcasting
Broadcasting is now more resilient — if the first broadcast times out, your transaction is automatically relayed through other peers, so your payments get through.

### 🔄 Faster and More Reliable Plugin Source Refresh
Plugin sources now refresh about 1 second after startup instead of much later, and downloads use a timeout scaled for proxy connections, so they no longer fail as often on slow networks.
