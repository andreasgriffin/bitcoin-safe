- 📅 **[Key health plugin](https://bitcoin-safe.org/en/plugins/keyhealth/)**
  - 📊 Keep track of when you last signed with a hardware signer
  - 💾 Sign messages with the hardware signer
- **Import wallets from other apps**
  - Import public wallet data and supported labels from Sparrow, Electrum, Wasabi, Specter Desktop, and Ledger Live.
- 🪙 **Use UTXOs from different sources**
  - Add UTXOs from open wallets or enter transaction outpoints manually.
  - Merge a PSBT to import all of its inputs and outputs.
- 📄 **PDF exports**
  - Export transactions and PSBTs with recipient details, fees, locktime, QR codes, and a transaction-flow diagram.
  - Export a PDF overview of open wallets and their shared signers. PDFs use the app's language.
- ⚡ **Sync and performance**
  - Compact-filter sync saves progress and can resume; transaction history updates while syncing.
  - Wallets open, close, and display transactions faster.
